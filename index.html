A bit about me

Hey! I'm a final-year B.E. Electronics & Communication student at Government Engineering College Bhavnagar (GTU). I started with simple Arduino LED projects and gradually moved into IoT devices, custom circuit boards, and sensor-driven systems.

What draws me to embedded systems is the feedback loop — you write code, flash it to a microcontroller, and something physical responds. That tangible connection between software and hardware is what keeps me engaged.

I'm currently seeking internship opportunities where I can learn from experienced engineers, contribute to real products, and grow as a hardware developer.

Solo Project · Jul – Aug 2025

Smart Gesture Glove

A wearable glove equipped with 5 flex sensors (one per finger) that translates hand gestures into readable text on a 16×2 LCD display in real time. Designed to assist speech-impaired individuals in daily communication. Each finger maps to a specific phrase — "I NEED FOOD", "I NEED WATER", "FOOTBALL KHEYLE", "HAAR MAAN LE", and more. Circuit designed and simulated in Tinkercad before hardware assembly.
